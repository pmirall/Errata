## Lo que ya existe
**The dex data layer is complete and costs 5 bytes of globals, measured.**

`Errata/src/game/dex.h:54-76` is the whole API: `dex_bind(uint8_t*)` / `dex_unbind()` / `dex_reset()`, `dex_mark_seen()` / `dex_mark_caught()` (both return "this call CHANGED something"), `dex_seen()` / `dex_caught()`, `dex_count_seen()` / `dex_count_caught()`. Two bits per species, 1-based ids, id 0 and anything past the roster answer false and write nothing (`Errata/src/game/dex.cpp:16-23`). `dex_count_*()` is already a 60-iteration loop over `get_bit()` (`Errata/src/game/dex.cpp:63-71`) — the idiom a family counter should copy rather than invent.

Storage is the caller's: 15 bytes, `DEX_BYTES` defined in `Errata/src/core/nt_types.h:425`, carved out of `ConfigV2.dex` and bound once at load in `Errata/src/persistence/game_state.cpp:246`. `Config` (v1) deliberately does NOT hold it — `Errata/src/core/nt_types.h:443-451` records that carving it out of `reserved_b` made a migrated save open with half the roster discovered, because the v1 fixture's "41.3874"/"2.1686" are perfectly good bits.

**The screen is 140 lines and holds one byte of state.** `Errata/src/ui/screen_dex.cpp:42` — `static uint8_t s_cur = 1`, a species id. Geometry, all asserted rather than eyeballed (`screen_dex.cpp:24-40`):

## Coste: globals 0 B, flash 800 B
**Zero, and measured rather than argued.** `riscv32-esp-elf-nm -C -S` over the two object files of the release build I just made:

- `sketch/src/ui/screen_dex.cpp.o` → `00000001 d s_cur` — the whole screen is **1 byte**, in `.data` because it initialises to 1.
- `sketch/src/game/dex.cpp.o` → `00000004 b s_bytes` — the whole module is **4 bytes**, the bound pointer.

So the entire on-device wiki costs **5 bytes of globals today**. The recommended scope adds no static: `dex_family_caught()`, `dex_family_seen()` and `dex_families_complete()` are pure functions over the already-bound `s_bytes` and over `constexpr` tables that live in flash, and the pips and objective line are pure functions of `s_cur` plus those readers. New `.text` and new `.rodata` do not touch `.dram0.*`.

Three measurements that fix what "a screen costs globals" actually means here, all from `nm -S` on `Errata.ino.elf`:

- `3c126eec 00000300 D SCREENS` — the 32-row `ScreenDef` table is 768 B at a **0x3c… DROM address**, i.e. flash-mapped `.rodata`, not globals. 768/32 = 24 = `sizeof(ScreenDef)`.
- `3c117328 00000080 d kScreenName` — 128 B, also flash. (budget.md quotes 116 B; it is 128 now.)
- `3fc94730 00000040 b s_frame_max` — 64 B at a **0x3fc… SRAM address**, i.e. real globals. That is `Errata/src/core/perf.cpp:15`, `uint16_t s_frame_max[SCR_COUNT]`.

**So the true globals price of a new `ScreenId` is exactly 2 bytes** (one `uint16_t` in `s_frame_max`) plus whatever statics the new screen keeps. It is cheap in bytes and expensive in guards. The recommended scope adds no `ScreenId`, so even those 2 bytes are not spent.

If the cut-for-later family view mode is ever added, it is **1 byte** (`static uint8_t s_view`), the same 1 byte `s_cur` measures at.
