# RECON A1

## Lo que ya existe
**The refusal is one `if`, and it is on the RECEIVE side only.**

`Errata/src/networking/protocol.cpp:126-132` — inside `pbw_decode()`, before the local `BugInstance` is built:
```
if (species > (uint8_t)SPECIES_ID_BUILTIN_MAX ||
    (flags & (uint8_t)(PBF_CUSTOM | PBF_HAS_CUSTOM_SPRITE)) != 0u)
  return VR_WIRE_CUSTOM_UNRESOLVED;
```
That is the whole gate. There is **no send-side gate at all**: `session_set_trade()` (`networking/session.cpp:328-341`) runs only `validate_bug()`, which accepts `PBF_CUSTOM|PBF_HAS_CUSTOM_SPRITE` (`game/validate.cpp:32-34` `VLD_FLAGS_MASK` includes both), and `pbw_encode()` writes `rec[BUGW_OFF_FLAGS] = p.flags` verbatim (`protocol.cpp:100`). `ui/screen_link.cpp:300-321` `pick_trade_slot()` asks `trade_offer_check()` (`game/trade.cpp:54-67`), which has no custom rule. So **today a player can already offer a drawn creature**; the peer answers `PT_TRADE_READY` with `verdict = VR_WIRE_CUSTOM_UNRESOLVED` and `trade_link.cpp:244-247` closes `SE_REJECTED`. The feature is not blocked, it is *broken at the far end*.

**What a custom sprite costs on the wire: 144 B, and the whole record is 192 B.**
`persistence/save_schema.h:481-515`: `CustomSpeciesRec` is 192 B — header 16, name 13, reserved 17, `sprite[2][72]` at offset 46 = 144 B, `crc16` at 190 over bytes 0..189. Measured `sizeof(CustomSpeciesRec) == 192`. `CS_SPRITE_BYTES 72`, `CS_SPRITE_FRAMES 2`, `CS_SPRITE_W/H 24` (`save_schema.h:469-477`). The sprite alone will not travel usefully without the stats/name/moves around it, so **the unit of transfer is the 192 B record, not the 144 B of pixels**.

**Why a wider record is impossible, confirmed.** `BUGW_BYTES == TR_WIRE_BYTES == 48` is static_asserted (`protocol.h:299-300`) against `PendingTrade.in_wire[48]` inside a frozen 64 B persisted blob (`save_schema.h:534-548`, four asserts). Nine spare bytes at `BUGW_OFF_RESERVED` (`protocol.h:324`). protocol.h:291-296 states the fix must be a separate message; that is correct.

**Storage: ten slots, projected rows + kept pixels, 1,440 B already spent.**
`game/species_custom.cpp:16-23`: `static SpeciesDef s_rows[10]` (240 B) + `static uint8_t s_bits[10][2][72]` (**1,440 B of globals, already paid**) + `uint16_t s_mask`. `csp_install(rec)` (`species_custom.cpp:39-86`) refuses via `validate_custom_species()` and otherwise projects `stage=1, family=0, compat_group=0, spawn_weight=0`, copies the 144 B, and sets the mask bit. `csp_free_slot()` (`:108-114`) returns the lowest clear mask bit or `CSP_SLOT_NONE`. `csp_slot_of(id) = id - CREATOR_SPECIES_ID_MIN` (`:116-122`). `species_get()` reaches it through the bound resolver `SPECIES_CUSTOM_RESOLVER` (`data/species_table.h:347-360`), so **no caller branches on "custom"** — that is the property the whole feature leans on.

Flash side: `persistence/save_manager.cpp:811-829` `custom_species_install_all()` rebuilds the registry on every load, *before* `quarantine_scan()` (`:770-781`); `save_custom_species()` (`:525-533`) writes key `cs<slot>`.

**Breeding a drawn creature is structurally impossible and not a wire problem.** `game/breeding.cpp:74-76` refuses `compat_group == 0` (`BRD_COMPAT_GROUP`), and `validate_custom_species()` refuses a non-zero one (`game/validate.cpp:422`, `VR_CS_BAD_COMPAT`). The child's species is derived from the parents' FAMILY and a custom species has none (`docs/decisions.md:2412-2427`). Nothing in `breed_link.cpp` can change that.

**What the receiver's registry does when full, today:** `creator_server.cpp:510-511` answers HTTP 409 `"csfull"`. There is **no way to free a slot**: `csp_forget()` has callers only in `creator_server.cpp` (error rollback). P8-C4's reclaim screen was never built — `grep` for a UI caller returns nothing. So "ten full" is permanent for a device.

**Budget, measured, not recalled.** `CHANGELOG.md:308-310` (the most recent Sizes line, the wiki commit): release **1,363,996 / 1,600,000 flash** and **61,276 / 65,000 globals** → **236,004 B of flash free and 3,724 B of globals free**. `docs/budget.md:1202-1203`'s 245,188 / 5,532 is the phase-10-exit figure and is stale by three commits.

## Riesgos
- **The pair CRC is the trap.** `trade_pair_crc()` (protocol.cpp:186-200) is crc16 over the two 48 B records in canonical order and both devices must get the same number, or every CONFIRM is dropped as wrong-pair. Remapping the species byte inside `tl.in_rec` — the obvious place — makes the two devices disagree and the trade dies SE_LOST with no reason a log reader could name. The remap MUST be into a scratch copy; `tl.in_rec`, `tl.in_crc` and the READY echo stay as received.
- **Flash ordering, and it is the power-cut one.** The cs record must land on flash BEFORE `trade_journal_received()` writes W2, or a reboot between the two decodes `in_wire` against a registry that has never heard of that species and `apply_committed()` (trade.cpp:156-167) rolls back a trade the peer may already have committed. The reverse residue — a cs record with no Bug — is an orphan slot and nothing worse, which is the same direction `creator_server.cpp:568-576` already chose.
- **A quarantined Bug can have its species stolen.** `quarantine_scan()` (save_manager.cpp:770-781) leaves a Bug whose cs record was lost sitting in the Box with `species_id = 203`. `csp_free_slot()` (species_custom.cpp:108-114) consults only the occupancy mask, so installing a stranger's creature into slot 3 silently gives that Bug the stranger's stats and body at the next load. This defect exists TODAY in `creator_server.cpp:510`; A1 makes it reachable from the outside, which changes its severity.
- **`DISC_CAP_MASK` is a compatibility cliff, not a gradient.** `discovery.cpp:87` and `:129` refuse a whole beacon carrying any bit outside the mask (`DE_CAPS`). Adding `DISC_CAP_CUSTOM` means a board flashed before this commit stops SEEING a board flashed after it — not merely stops trading custom creatures with it. Nothing has shipped and the bench has two boards from one tree, so this is acceptable; it must be in the commit message rather than found on the bench.
- **`ProtoCaps.max_payload` is compared for strict equality** (`session.cpp:296`, `SCW_PAYLOAD_MAX`). The chunked design leaves it at 148 and this stays inert. The single-frame design would move it to 196 and make every mixed-firmware session die SE_INCOMPATIBLE — a second reason beyond the 1,536 B.
- **`origin` is overwritten.** `apply_committed()` sets `in.origin = ORIGIN_TRADED` (trade.cpp:190), so the receiver's copy loses ORIGIN_CREATOR. `PBF_CUSTOM` survives. Whether the bestiary should say 'drawn by somebody else' is a guide question the agent has to be told about.
- **A cs slot the sender no longer needs is never freed.** After B1 clears the outgoing slot (trade.cpp:169-183) the sender's `cs<n>` record stays on flash with no Bug pointing at it, and there is no reclaim UI anywhere in `src/ui/` — P8-C4's screen was never built. Trade away ten drawn creatures and the creator says `csfull` for ever. This is a pre-existing hole that A1 makes ten times easier to fall into.
- **The stack, not the heap.** `session_poll()` already holds `uint8_t buf[164]` and `on_frame()` a 160 B ProtoMsg. `load_def` puts a 192 B CustomSpeciesRec on the stack at every ladder rung for a custom offer. Comfortable inside the 8 KB loop task, but it is the deepest frame in the radio path and nothing in this tree measures stack.
- **The 24x24 sprite geometry is now a wire contract.** `CS_SPRITE_W/H` (save_schema.h:476-477) reaching a peer means changing it becomes a protocol break, not just a save break. Say so beside the constants.

## Incógnitas bloqueantes

The ring refuses the NEWEST on full (rxring.h:63-72) and the ladder recovers, but nothing here has ever run on a radio — `docs/budget.md` §16.2 says so in as many words.

A product decision, not a code one.

## Alcance recomendado
**A good one-commit version: `net: the creature you drew can change hands`.**

One operation, one direction of ambition: **TRADE ONLY**, both ends, over the existing five wire steps, with the journal and the power-cut sweep intact. Concretely — `PT_SPECIES_DEF` in two 96 B parts so `PROTO_PAYLOAD_MAX` never moves; `pbw_decode()` accepting a custom id the receiver can resolve and *deriving* `custom_sprite` instead of believing it; the receiver installing into its own slot and remapping the species in a scratch copy before the journal; `csp_find_same()` so a creature that comes home reuses its slot; `TDR_NO_CUSTOM_SLOT` when the ten are full, refused before a single byte is installed or journalled; `DISC_CAP_CUSTOM` so the UI never offers a drawn creature to a peer that cannot take one; three Spanish strings; the guide agent last.

1. **Breeding.** Not deferred — *impossible at this layer*. `compat_group == 0` is refused by `breeding.cpp:74-76` and a non-zero one is refused by `validate_custom_species()` (`validate.cpp:422`); the child's species is derived from a FAMILY a custom species does not have (`decisions.md:2412-2427`). 3. **A cs-slot reclaim screen.** P8-C4's never-built reclaim is the reason "ten full" is permanent. It is a `ScreenId`, a menu row, a confirm and a golden — a separate commit under the guide rule, and mixing it in doubles the guide surface.

4. **Sweeping orphan cs records after a trade.** Tempting (a slot with no Bug pointing at it is dead weight) and wrong to bundle: it is a rule about the whole registry, it interacts with quarantined Bugs, and it wants its own sweep test. Leave the orphan, name it in the header.

5. **A new screen or a progress indicator for the transfer.** Two frames on a 1 s ladder. A toast on refusal is the whole UI. Any screen spends from the same 3,724 B this feature is trying to stay small inside.

Net: **~200 B of globals (5.4 % of 3,724) and roughly 6,000 B of flash (2.5 % of 236,004)** — calibrated against `ecce310`, the breeding-wire commit, which was 450 lines, four new types, a new session state, new hooks and a UI wire-up, and measured **+5,844 flash / +16 globals**. The +16 there and the +200 here differ by exactly one thing: `breed_link` stores no peer artefact and this stores a 192 B one.

## Coste: globals 200 B, flash 6000 B
**Measured, by compiling the real headers** (`g++ -std=c++17 -I Errata/src` over `networking/trade_link.h`): `sizeof(TradeLink) == 168`, `sizeof(Session) == 376`, `sizeof(CustomSpeciesRec) == 192`, `sizeof(ProtoMsg) == 160`.

The firmware holds **exactly one** `TradeLink`, file-scope at `ui/screen_link.cpp:73` (`static TradeLink s_tl;`). Adding `in_def[192]` + one hook pointer (4) + four bookkeeping bytes takes it from 168 to ~368 → **+200 B, once**, with a little alignment slack either way.

`ProtoMsg` does **not** grow: measured, a 100 B union member sits under `ProtoTeamSubmit`'s 148, so `sizeof(ProtoMsg)` stays 160. Everything else the change adds is code and `.rodata` (Spanish strings and their pointer table), which is flash on this part — `CHANGELOG.md:1013-1022` and `docs/budget.md` §15.3 both record exactly that: seven new strings and their table pointers moved flash and left the globals line unchanged.

**The expensive design I am rejecting, priced.** Sending the 192 B record in ONE frame is the obvious shape and it costs **+1,536 B of globals**, not 200. Payload would be 4+192 = 196, so `PROTO_PAYLOAD_MAX` 148 → 196 and `PROTO_FRAME_MAX` 164 → 212 (still inside the 250 B ESP-NOW datagram, `protocol.h:222-224`). The session receive ring is `static uint8_t s_rx_bytes[LINK_RX_SLOTS * PROTO_FRAME_MAX]` (`transport_espnow.cpp:75`) with `LINK_RX_SLOTS 32` (`core/config.h:585`): 32 × 164 = 5,248 → 32 × 212 = 6,784. **+1,536 B = 41 % of the 3,724 B free, for message-framing convenience.** Chunking into two 96 B parts keeps `PROTO_PAYLOAD_MAX` at 148 and that ring at 5,248 bytes, and moves the staging cost to one 192 B buffer in a struct there is exactly one of.

So: **200 B of 3,724 (5.4 %)**, against a rejected alternative at 1,536 B (41 %). The `static_assert(PROTO_PAYLOAD_MAX == 148)` at `protocol.h:209` is what makes the cheap design the one that compiles.
