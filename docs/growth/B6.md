THE GAME ITSELF — CUOTA (QUOTA): the decision game the set lacks.
Five visible jobs, each with a printed COST and a printed VALUE, and a bandwidth budget strictly smaller than the sum of the costs. L walks the caret; R toggles the job under it in or out of the plan. The run ends when the player commits (R on the last, CONFIRM row) or at a 12,000 ms deadline. `finish()` returns `1000 * value_taken / value_of_the_best_legal_plan`, where the optimum is computed at `init()` by exhausting 32 subsets and cached in one byte.

Why this and not another fuse game: it is the only shape in which NO press has a deadline shorter than the run, the score is a pure function of the set you chose, and the optimum is knowable by the player from what is on the panel. `init()` rejection-samples the instance until the left-to-right greedy plan is strictly worse than the optimum, which is what makes "think" beat "mash R" by construction rather than by hope — the same technique delete_logic.cpp:83-88 uses to force contested targets.

`struct QuotaState`: `uint8_t cost[5]`, `uint8_t value[5]`, `uint8_t taken_mask`, `uint8_t cursor`, `uint8_t budget`, `uint8_t spent`, `uint8_t best` (optimum value) = 15 B of 32. No `note` (no toast, no new string beyond the two).

Name "CUOTA" (5 of 12 chars). Hint "Elige qué paquetes pasan." (25 of 25 — count it before committing; strings_es.h:334-338 is a hard limit because the intro card does not wrap).
