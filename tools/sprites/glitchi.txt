# Family 16 GLITCH motif: a SOLID BLOCK cut into horizontal scanline slices and
# each slice pushed sideways, with ONE EYE STRADDLING A SEAM so the displacement
# is legible on the face and not only on the outline. Growth is by slice count
# and by how far they travel: three slices and 2px, six slices and one slice
# missing altogether, then eleven slices, 5px throws and torn bands.
# The briefs for this family ask stage 2 for DITHERED slices. Dither is drawn
# here as chunky 4-on/2-off tearing instead: a 1px checkerboard on a 1-bit
# panel is a grey smear that flickers between the two frames, which is exactly
# what this phase forbids, while a torn band still reads as broken data.
#
# GLITCHI - species 46, family 16 stage 0, type CORRUPT (GLITCH / EVASIVE).
# 'Baila donde no deberia.' - it dances where it has no business dancing.
# root:       horizontal scanline slices offset sideways
# silhouette: 10x10 square in three slices, the middle offset 2px right, one
#             eye split across the seam.
# frame 2:    the offset flips to the left.
# The eye is one 2x2 hole drawn BEFORE the slice is displaced, so its top half
# stays with the head and its bottom half rides the middle slice 2px away. That
# is the whole family in one feature: the tear is on the face.
# Only the middle slice moves between frames. Two thirds of the body is pixel
# for pixel identical, so the flip reads as a twitch and not as a redraw.
