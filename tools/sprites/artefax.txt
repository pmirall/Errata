# right and 3px up, which fuses each of them into a neighbour - that smear is
# the artifact, not a placement bug. The eyes sit in the two undisplaced
# blocks of the middle row, so the face survives the tear. Frame 2 jitters the
# torn blocks 1px rather than jumping them to the opposite diagonal: the jump
# moves 96 px and reads as a second drawing.
# A blink here is a SQUINT (2x2 hole -> 2x1), never a shut eye: the two frames alternate at even duty, so a fully closed eye reads as flicker.
