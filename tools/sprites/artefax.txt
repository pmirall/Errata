# 'It stains everything you look at.'
# root:       chunky discrete blocks dissolving along a diagonal
# silhouette: a 14x8 block head with two sockets over a split 12px body, its
#             shoulder torn away in two 4x4 blocks stepping up and to the right.
# frame 2:    the two torn blocks jitter 1px along the diagonal and the right
#             foot steps 1px out.
# Family motif: chunky discrete blocks with 1px seams, dissolving along a diagonal. Same block language at 2, 3 and 5 blocks across.
#
# REDRAWN AT P9-C6 FOR TWO DEFECTS, both of which its sibling file already
# names and fixes.
#
# (1) THE EYES WERE THE FAILED CONSTRUCTION. They were 2x2 holes punched into
# 4x4 blocks, which leaves a ONE PIXEL wall. pixio.txt records exactly that as
# already-failed - "its two eyes sat in a 5px block where a 2x2 hole leaves a
# 1px wall, so at panel scale the eye row dissolved" - and Pixio was redrawn at
# 7px blocks to fix it. This file was not, and still claimed "the face survives
# the tear". The sockets now sit in a 14px band with a 2px wall left, 4px
# between and 4px right, above and below 2 solid rows: the same construction
# Pixio uses.
#
# (2) IT HAD THE WORST SILHOUETTE COHESION IN THE ROSTER: 128 px in four
# 8-connected pieces of 40/40/16/16, no dominant mass, which at 1x is confetti
# rather than a creature - and 128 px was LESS than its own stage 0 (Pixio 152),
# one of only two families whose ink fell at stage 1. The tear is still there
# and is still the identity, but it is drawn as a STEPPED SHOULDER: the two torn
# blocks touch the mass they came off, so the body is one 173 px piece and the
# family reads 152 -> 173 -> 201.
#
# DEVIATION FROM THE PACK: species.json's _silhouette says '14x14 of nine
# blocks, three of them displaced 3px along the diagonal'. Nine 4x4 blocks on a
# 5px pitch is what produced the confetti; two torn blocks off a solid head
# carries the same idea and survives the panel. Either the pack text gets
# corrected to match or this art does - they disagree in writing.
#
# A blink here is a SQUINT (2x2 hole -> 2x1), never a shut eye: the two frames
# alternate at even duty, so a fully closed eye reads as flicker.
