# Family 19 - PROBE. Family motif: TWO OVALS side by side, overlapping enough
# to be one body but never enough to stop being two - the creature always has
# two heads and the roster's flavour lines are always about a second one
# answering. Every stage widens the pair and grows the stem between them:
# none, then one, then two of different heights.
# The pair also carries the eye count: two, then two hollow cores, then four.
#
# BEAKON - species 56, family 19 stage 1, type SYSTEM (PROBE / FAST).
# 'It shouts its own name without stopping.'
# root:       two interlocking ovals
# silhouette: two 11x8 ovals interlocked, one drawn solid and one hollow, a
#             4px stem between them.
# frame 2:    solid and hollow swap.
#
# DEVIATION, stated plainly. A literal solid/hollow swap inverts about 80 of
# this body's 150 lit pixels every 420 ms. On a 1-bit panel at even duty that
# is not an animation, it is a strobe, and the two frames stop reading as one
# creature. What swaps here is the CORE: both heads are 2px rings, and the lit
# 3x2 signal inside one of them jumps to the other. 12 px move, the silhouette
# never changes, and the reading - only one of the two is transmitting, and it
# keeps changing which - is the one the flavour line and Twinix both want.
# The hollows are 5px wide with a 2px gap between them: taken any wider they
# meet across the 4px overlap and the two rings become one hollow.
# The stem runs down to y16, where the two ovals have not met yet, so it fills
# the notch between the heads instead of sitting on top of it.
