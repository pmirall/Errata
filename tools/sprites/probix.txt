# Family 19 - PROBE. Family motif: TWO OVALS side by side, overlapping enough
# to be one body but never enough to stop being two - the creature always has
# two heads and the roster's flavour lines are always about a second one
# answering. Every stage widens the pair and grows the stem between them:
# none, then one, then two of different heights.
# The pair also carries the eye count: two, then two hollow cores, then four.
#
# PROBIX - species 55, family 19 stage 0, type SYSTEM (PROBE / FAST).
# 'Is anybody there?'
# root:       two interlocking ovals
# silhouette: two 8x6 ovals overlapping 3px, one dot eye in each.
# frame 2:    the two eyes blink alternately.
#
# The ovals are 9x7, not 8x6: a 3x3 eye is the smallest unlit hole that can
# hold a SQUINT (3x3 -> 3x1) rather than a full shut, and a 6-row oval has no
# room to put one in with a rim left over. A fully shut eye would be right,
# but the two frames alternate at even duty every 420 ms, so an eye that shuts
# completely does not blink - it strobes.
# Blinking the two eyes ALTERNATELY rather than together is what sells the
# pair as two heads and not one body with two eyes; it is the same idea the
# whole family is built on, at the smallest scale it fits.
# The ovals part again at the top and bottom row, so the baby has two feet.
