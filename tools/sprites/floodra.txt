# Family 15 PING motif: a FAT OPEN CRESCENT whose mouth faces right - a disc
# with a second disc bitten out of it, so the arc is solid and comes to a point
# at both tips and never closes into a ring - with the FOCUS DOT sitting in the
# mouth as the creature's eye, and two stub feet. Growth adds rank after rank
# outside the shell: one emitted ping, then three, then five plus barbs, and
# the shell itself gains grooves so it reads as nested arcs too.
# Ranks are 2px solid with 2px gaps and never 1px with 1px gaps: at 1x on the
# panel a 1px/1px comb is a grey field, which is the thing this phase forbids.
#
# FLOODRA - species 44, family 15 stage 1, type SIGNAL (PING / FAST).
# 'Pregunta mil veces.' - it asks a thousand times.
# root:       open arcs, never closing into a ring
# silhouette: four arcs, the outer one broken into three dashes, the dot grown
#             to a 4px eye.
# frame 2:    the arcs step outward one rank and the innermost is dropped.
# Four ranks: a grooved shell (two), one solid ping, one broken into dashes.
# The focus dot is now a 5x5 eye with a 2x2 pupil - the same feature as PINGO's,
# grown, which is what keeps the two readable as one creature.
# frame 1 steps the two emitted ranks out 1px and shuts the pupil one row: the
# shell and the eye hold still, so the motion reads as a ripple leaving the
# body rather than as the body jumping.
