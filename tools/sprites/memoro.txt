# Family 13 MEMORY motif: a SOLID VERTICAL SLAB with a comb of contact notches
# bitten out of its bottom edge, a 1px waist notched into both side walls, and
# one 2x2 unlit eye per slab. Growth is by slab count - one, two, three - and
# the bottom edge decays across the three stages: clean comb, clean comb,
# then a melt. Eyes are unlit holes, never lit dots: the body is a solid
# silhouette, so a lit eye inside it is invisible.
#
# MEMORO - species 38, family 13 stage 1, type SYSTEM (MEMORY / BRUISER).
# 'Se acuerda de todo.' - it remembers everything.
# root:       vertical notched stick melting DOWNWARD
# silhouette: two sticks side by side bridged at the top, eight notches, small
#             feet.
# frame 2:    the bridge bar slides 1px left and right.
# The bridge makes the pair one silhouette - an arch - so the growth from one
# slab to two is a shape change and not just a duplicate. Eight notches, four
# per slab, on the same 2px pitch as KACHI so the comb is the same comb.
# The waist notch and the eye hole are carried over unchanged.
