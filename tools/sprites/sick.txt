# SICK - THE ONE ILL BODY, drawn at P9-C3.
#
# Replaces SICK_CHILD/TEEN/ADULT (28, 32 and 40 px).  As with SLEEP, the legacy
# lookup ignored `form` here too, so every species above CHILD already fell ill
# as the same drawing; and a sick BABY drew its own idle body, which was a
# silent hole in the old table.  Now every stage, baby included, draws this.
#
# A slumped lump, wider at the base than at the head, with sunken hole eyes, a
# flat mouth and three specks of contagion drifting over it.  It is NOT drawn
# with a germ glyph on the body: ui/actfx.cpp already floats EMO_GERM beside the
# pet during the medicine film, and two germs would read as decoration.
#
# The three specks are 2px wide, not 1: gen_sprites.py's own hairline count
# calls a pixel that is isolated in both axes DIRT at 1x, and taking that
# advice on the body this chunk drew is the least it can do.
#
# Frame 1 half-closes the eyes (a squint, never a shut eye - at a 50 % duty
# cycle a fully shut eye reads as flicker) and drifts each speck one pixel.
