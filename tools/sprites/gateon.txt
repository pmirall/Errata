# GATEON - species 35, family 12 stage 1, type SYSTEM (FIREWALL / TANK,
# COMMON). 'It decides who gets through today.'
# root:       broad pentagon shield
# silhouette: 16x14 shield, horizontal visor slit, two side flanges.
# frame 2:    the visor slides open to show three dots.
# Family motif: a broad PENTAGON SHIELD - flat top, straight sides, a taper to a 2px tip on the floor - carrying one unlit VISOR. The visor turns: a one-way vertical slit, then a horizontal band that lights up, then a lit band behind crenellations and a flame crest.
# DEVIATION: the visor does not open, it LIGHTS UP - the slit is the same
# shape in both frames and three 2x2 lamps come on inside it. An opening
# visor is a silhouette change every tick, which reads as a glitch; three
# lamps blinking on inside a fixed slot reads as a thing deciding, which is
# what the flavour line says it does. The lamps are 2x2 and not 2x1: a
# one-row lamp inside a dark slot is a hairline at 1x. The shoulders are
# chamfered 2px, which is not in the brief either: rendered flat, a 16px top
# edge over a taper reads as an envelope and not as a shield.

#
# P9-C3 INTEGRATION FIX: THIS BODY WAS EXACTLY SYMMETRIC ABOUT ITS OWN CENTRE
# COLUMN, so ui/xbm_mirror.cpp produced a byte-identical picture and the two
# fighters on the battle field faced the same way.  Nothing in the pipeline can
# see that - the mirror still ran, the ink count is the same, the bounding box
# is the same - and it was found by a test that renders the upright and the
# mirrored body and requires them to differ (tests/test_screens.cpp,
# every_species_draws_on_the_battle_field_without_clipping).  Spec 18/19 wants
# asymmetry anyway; this is the smallest edit that gives the body a facing.
# GATEON: the visor band is 5px of shoulder on the left and 6px on the right,
# and frame 1 lights TWO lamps rather than three centred ones.
