# BAKDORA (species 51) - family 17 PORT, stage 2.  REDRAWN AT P9-C3 INTEGRATION.
#
# WHY IT WAS REDRAWN.  The first version put nine 2px through-holes in a 17px
# slab.  At 1x, 2px holes with 2px walls are not holes - they are vertical
# stripes - so the family's one idea (a wall you can see the BACKGROUND through,
# and the gaps between the doorways are the legs) was gone, and what was left was
# a bright blob with a diagonal tail.  Its frame-2 'smaller shape in the doorway'
# was legible at 4x and invisible at 1x.
#
# WHAT IT IS NOW.  Skanor's wall, grown and broken open: four 2px pillars and
# three 3px doorways cut down to the floor (the same pitch Skanor uses, so the
# two stages share a foot), two 2x2 eye holes above them, and ONE big tear
# through the right edge with the ripped-off door hanging outside it on a 1px
# hinge.  One large hole instead of nine small ones is the whole change: at 24px
# a hole has to be 3px wide before the eye reads it as a hole.
#
# The door swings one row in frame 1 and an eye squints; the 'figure standing in
# the doorway' is dropped rather than drawn at a size where it cannot be seen.
