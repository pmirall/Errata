# KADENAX (species 14) - family 5 SPAM, stage 1.  REDRAWN AT P9-C3 INTEGRATION.
#
# WHY IT WAS REDRAWN.  The first version was seven plates whose links bridged
# whole rows (8-12 and 19-21 ran the full width), so the chain fused: rendered
# beside Spamito it read as a circuit board, not as separate flats, and its 6px
# clasp and its face were both lost inside 298 ink pixels.  The family's whole
# idea is that a spam creature is a CHAIN of linked flats and never one body, and
# that idea did not survive.
#
# WHAT IT IS NOW.  Spamito's plate, grown: five plates snaking an S from the head
# at top right down to a wide base plate on the floor, each 11px wide against
# Spamito's 7, each carrying a knocked-out line of junk text, and joined by 2px
# links with real background on both sides.  The head keeps Spamito's face - two
# square eye holes and a mouth slot - so the stage 0 and stage 1 heads are the
# same head.  Ink drops from 298 to 203 px, which is what makes the gaps read.
#
# DEVIATION: the pack says seven plates with two of them doubled.  Seven plates
# plus six links inside 24 rows leaves under 3 rows per plate, and a 2-row plate
# cannot hold a knockout, so this is five.  The clasp is dropped rather than
# drawn at 6px where it cannot be seen.  Frame 1 clacks the head one pixel and
# shifts two knockouts; the plates below it do not move, so it reads as one
# chain jerking rather than as two drawings.
