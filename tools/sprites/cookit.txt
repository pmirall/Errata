# Family 20 - COOKIE. Family motif: an EYE ON A STALK. The body is a stalk,
# never legs and never a torso; everything else is eyes budded off it, and
# every stage adds eyes rather than bulk. The stalk stands on a small pad, and
# by the last stage the pad has come apart into loose dots.
# An eye here is always the same three rings: a 2px lit rim, an unlit sclera
# inside it, and a LIT pupil inside that. The pupil is what moves - it is the
# only moving part in all three stages, and it is why the family reads as
# watching rather than as breathing.
#
# COOKIT - species 58, family 20 stage 0, type SIGNAL (COOKIE / FAST).
# 'A crumb that follows you.'
# root:       eye on a stalk - tall and thin, never legs
# silhouette: 3px stalk 8px tall, a 9px round eye on top, a small 7px base.
# frame 2:    the pupil glances one pixel down and to the right.
#
# THE EYE WAS REDRAWN AT P9-C6 AND THE REASON IS THE FAMILY'S OWN RULE.
# The first version put a 7px eye on the stalk, which leaves 3px of interior -
# not enough for a 2px pupil AND an unlit ring around it. What was drawn instead
# was a 2px unlit NOTCH sliding across a lit blob, and the P9 exit review said
# what that costs: the pupil is fused to the rim in BOTH frames, so no frame of
# the family's stage 0 contains a readable eye, and 'watching rather than
# breathing' - the sentence at the top of this file - does not survive at the
# stage a player meets first. It read as a pin or a lollipop.
#
# The eye is 9px now: a 2px lit rim, a 5x5 unlit sclera, and a 2x2 LIT pupil
# with a one-pixel unlit ring on every side IN BOTH FRAMES. That ring is the
# whole point and it is why the eye had to grow. 64 ink px became 92, which also
# takes this body off the bottom of the roster (it was the lightest of the 60).
#
# The pupil is drawn LIT inside an unlit sclera, not as an unlit hole in a lit
# eye: an unlit pupil cannot move, because the sclera around it would have to be
# lit too and there is no room for four rings.
#
# The stalk lost a row to the taller eye (8, not 9) and the pad is 7px at the
# floor and 5px above it, wider than the pack's 4px: a 4px pad under a 3px stalk
# is not a foot, it is the stalk with a step in it.
