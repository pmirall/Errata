# Family 18 - CRYPTO. Family motif: an angular LOZENGE - a bevelled body that
# is widest across the middle and tapers at both ends - carrying a SHACKLE
# arch on top and a KEYHOLE on its face. All three stages are that padlock:
# shut, then faceted and double-locked, then broken open with the shackle
# snapped and the keyhole gone through to nothing.
# The keyhole is on the body's centre line under the eyes, so it reads as a
# mouth: a padlock with only a keyhole is an object, and the roster wants a
# creature. The eyes are unlit holes; the keyhole is an unlit hole too, but a
# wider one with a slot dropped out of its bottom, which is what tells them
# apart at 1x.
#
# RANSORA - species 54, family 18 stage 2, type CORRUPT (CRYPTO / GLASS).
# 'It gives it back if you pay.'
# root:       angular lozenge with a shackle
# silhouette: 19x19 lozenge, the shackle snapped and hanging, the keyhole
#             opened into a black hole with three chains dragging out.
# frame 2:    the chains reel in 2px and one of them snaps taut.
#
# Sections 18/19 want the final stage bigger, sharper and more asymmetric, so
# every symmetry the family had is broken here: the shackle's crown is snapped
# short on one side and its other arm hangs off the shoulder, the two eyes are
# different widths at different heights, and the keyhole has eaten the bottom
# of the body so the lozenge stands on two ragged stumps instead of a base.
# The chains are what touch the floor, not the body - they hang out of the
# hole and drag, which is the whole idea of the creature. They are drawn as
# 2px links with 1px gaps: a chain is the one thing at this size that a broken
# line reads as, and it is the only place in these twelve bodies where an
# unlit gap inside a lit shape is meant to be seen as a gap.
