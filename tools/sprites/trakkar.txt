# Family 20 - COOKIE. Family motif: an EYE ON A STALK. The body is a stalk,
# never legs and never a torso; everything else is eyes budded off it, and
# every stage adds eyes rather than bulk. The stalk stands on a small pad, and
# by the last stage the pad has come apart into loose dots.
# An eye here is always the same three rings: a 2px lit rim, an unlit sclera
# inside it, and a LIT pupil inside that. The pupil is what moves - it is the
# only moving part in all three stages, and it is why the family reads as
# watching rather than as breathing.
#
# TRAKKAR - species 59, family 20 stage 1, type SIGNAL (COOKIE / FAST).
# 'It knows where you have been.'
# root:       eye on a stalk - tall and thin, never legs
# silhouette: 3px stalk 14px tall with two side stalks, three eyes of
#             different sizes.
# frame 2:    the three pupils converge on one point and separate.
#
# Cookit's eye, kept whole on top, with two more budded off the stalk at
# different heights and different sizes - 7x7, 7x5 and 5x5 - so the growth
# reads as the same creature sprouting rather than as a new shape. The side
# stalks are at unequal heights on purpose: level, the body reads as a symbol.
# Each side stalk meets its eye BELOW the eye's widest row, so the eye's top
# rows stand clear of the stalk with unlit between them. Attached at the
# widest row, eye and branch and stalk fuse into one bar and the eye stops
# being an eye on a stalk at all.
# In frame 0 the three pupils point outward, in frame 1 all three swing toward
# the same spot. 14 px move and the silhouette never changes; on this family
# the pupils are the whole performance.
