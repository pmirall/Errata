# Family 18 - CRYPTO. Family motif: an angular LOZENGE - a bevelled body that
# is widest across the middle and tapers at both ends - carrying a SHACKLE
# arch on top and a KEYHOLE on its face. All three stages are that padlock:
# shut, then faceted and double-locked, then broken open with the shackle
# snapped and the keyhole gone through to nothing.
# The keyhole is on the body's centre line under the eyes, so it reads as a
# mouth: a padlock with only a keyhole is an object, and the roster wants a
# creature. The eyes are unlit holes; the keyhole is an unlit hole too, but a
# wider one with a slot dropped out of its bottom, which is what tells them
# apart at 1x.
#
# KLAVIK - species 52, family 18 stage 0, type SYSTEM (CRYPTO / GLASS).
# 'It keeps your secret well.'
# root:       angular lozenge with a shackle
# silhouette: 9x11 lozenge, a 4px semicircular shackle on top, one keyhole
#             pixel.
# frame 2:    the shackle lifts 1px and drops.
#
# The pack asks for ONE keyhole pixel. A single unlit pixel in the middle of a
# 9-wide lit body is a dropout, not a feature - at 1x it is the same thing the
# panel does when a pixel is stuck. It is drawn instead as a 3px round top
# with a 2px slot under it (5 unlit pixels), which is the smallest shape that
# still reads as a keyhole rather than as damage.
# The whole shackle translates one row for frame 2: 14 px change, all of it on
# the outline, so the idle reads as the lock giving a little tug at itself.
