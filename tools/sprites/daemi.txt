# DEVIATIONS. (1) The base is 14px and the top step 10px, not a 10px base.

# 1px wall between a slot and the edge reads as dirt at 1x rather than as a
# face. (2) There is a 4px cap on top and a notch between two feet at the
# bottom, neither in the brief. Rendered as a bare two-step block it is a
