# Family 19 - PROBE. Family motif: TWO OVALS side by side, overlapping enough
# to be one body but never enough to stop being two - the creature always has
# two heads and the roster's flavour lines are always about a second one
# answering. Every stage widens the pair and grows the stem between them:
# none, then one, then two of different heights.
# The pair also carries the eye count: two, then two hollow cores, then four.
#
# TWINIX - species 57, family 19 stage 2, type SIGNAL (PROBE / FAST).
# 'It passes itself off as your network.'
# root:       two interlocking ovals
# silhouette: two 15x11 ovals overlapped into a figure-eight, the shared
#             region left empty, four eyes in all.
# frame 2:    the empty shared region slides along the eight, so which twin
#             is real keeps changing.
#
# 24px wide, edge to edge - the widest of these twelve, which is how sections
# 18/19 get their bigger final stage out of a family whose root shape is two
# ovals. The sharper and the asymmetric come from the two barbs: same motif as
# Beakon's single stem, but one is 6px and the other is 3px, so nothing about
# this body is mirror-symmetric even though its root shape is.
# The void is a 5x7 block, not the true lens where the two ovals intersect.
# Clearing the real lens severs the body into two loose ovals; a block that
# stops two rows short at each end leaves the ovals bridged top and bottom, so
# the outline is a genuine figure-eight with a hole in it, and the hole is
# free to slide 2px along the waist without opening the ring.
# The four eyes are stacked in pairs at the OUTER ends of the heads. Side by
# side they leave a 1px lit sliver between them, and the same sliver appears
# between an eye and the void as it slides past - a 1px lit island is noise.
