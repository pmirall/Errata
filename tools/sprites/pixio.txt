# PIXIO (species 10) - family 4 PIXEL, stage 0.  REDRAWN AT P9-C3 INTEGRATION.
#
# WHY IT WAS REDRAWN.  The first version was three 5px blocks and a loose crumb.
# Rendered beside the other 59 it read as an ICON, not an animal: no feet, no
# mouth, and its two eyes sat in a 5px block where a 2x2 hole leaves a 1px wall,
# so at panel scale the eye row dissolved.  It was also the most static body in
# the roster.  A player meets this one FIRST in its family, so it has to read.
#
# WHAT IT IS NOW.  The family's 2x2 arrangement of chunky blocks with 1px seams,
# at 7px per block so a 2x2 eye hole keeps a 2px wall all round.  The
# bottom-left block is the one that has dissolved (the family's diagonal), and
# what is left of it is a crumb sitting on the floor.  Two eye holes in the top
# blocks, a mouth slot in the bottom-right block, and two 2px feet under it so
# the thing stands instead of hovering.  The seam between the right-hand blocks
# is broken by a 3px bridge: a full-width 1px seam splits the silhouette in two
# at 1x, which is the failure this body already had once.
#
# _frame2 ('the missing corner rotates to the next corner') is NOT implemented as
# written: at 7px blocks that relocates 49 px of a 130 px body every other tick,
# which is two drawings, not an idle.  Frame 1 blinks (a squint, never a shut
# eye), opens the mouth one pixel each way, and drifts both crumbs one pixel.
