# PANIKA (species 48) - family 16 GLITCH, stage 2.  REDRAWN AT P9-C3 INTEGRATION.
#
# WHY IT WAS REDRAWN.  The first version was eleven 1-2 row slices with 5px
# throws and three torn bands.  It was the only body in the roster that carried
# its identity in TEXTURE, and texture is the weakest thing to carry identity
# with at 24px on one bit: rendered at 1x it flattened into television static and
# the head block it did have was lost inside it.  Nothing in the pipeline can see
# that - it passes every byte check - so it was found by looking.
#
# WHAT IT IS NOW.  The same family vocabulary (a solid block cut into scanline
# slices pushed sideways, an eye straddling a seam) with the slice count cut from
# eleven to FIVE and each slice 3-4 rows deep, so the silhouette is a legible
# ragged staircase instead of a field.  The head is the top two slices, thrown
# 2px against each other, and the eye holes run ACROSS that throw - which is the
# family's whole idea and was invisible before.  One torn band survives (the
# middle slice, broken into three chunks); the rest are solid.
#
# The pack's _silhouette asks for three DITHERED slices.  Dither is what this
# atlas forbids at 24px, so those are torn instead - 4-5px of ink, 1px of gap -
# and the count is one band, not three.  Frame 1 steps one slice 2px, drifts two
# chunks of the torn band and squints; the pack's 'swap two 5px offsets' redraws
# a third of the body between frames, which is two drawings, not an idle.
