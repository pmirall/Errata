# Family 18 - CRYPTO. Family motif: an angular LOZENGE - a bevelled body that
# is widest across the middle and tapers at both ends - carrying a SHACKLE
# arch on top and a KEYHOLE on its face. All three stages are that padlock:
# shut, then faceted and double-locked, then broken open with the shackle
# snapped and the keyhole gone through to nothing.
# The keyhole is on the body's centre line under the eyes, so it reads as a
# mouth: a padlock with only a keyhole is an object, and the roster wants a
# creature. The eyes are unlit holes; the keyhole is an unlit hole too, but a
# wider one with a slot dropped out of its bottom, which is what tells them
# apart at 1x.
#
# CIFRAX - species 53, family 18 stage 1, type SYSTEM (CRYPTO / GLASS).
# 'Nobody reads what it writes.'
# root:       angular lozenge with a shackle
# silhouette: 13x15 lozenge cut into four facets, a doubled shackle, the
#             keyhole grown into a 3px slot.
# frame 2:    the facets shift one step around the lozenge, like a dial
#             turning.
#
# The four facets are unlit cuts taken out of the RIM, at 12/3/6/9 in frame 0
# and on the four diagonals in frame 1, so the turn is a 45-degree step that
# changes the OUTLINE - which is the only kind of change a 24px body reliably
# shows at 1x. 16 px move, and they all move together, so it reads as a dial.
# Two earlier attempts were rendered and thrown away. Facets as full unlit
# diameters rotating from + to X repaint a quarter of the interior every
# 420 ms: that is a redraw, not an idle. Facets as short seams radiating from
# the keyhole leave a single lit pixel stranded between each seam and the
# keyhole, and a 1px lit island between two unlit fields is indistinguishable
# from noise at 1x - the same trap that makes a 1px keyhole unreadable.
# The doubled shackle is one wide arch with a narrower arch nested inside it
# and 1px unlit channels between them; both arches have 2px legs, because a
# 1px leg flanked by unlit is four thin lines at 1x, not two arches.
